# TechByGrace services and pricing

## How pricing works
All prices are starting prices. Every project gets a fixed quote after a short free scoping call, based on its features and timeline. Prices are in Nigerian naira for clients in Nigeria and in US dollars for international clients.

## Business websites
Responsive websites for businesses and personal brands, built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript or React and Next.js. Includes up to 5 pages, contact form, basic SEO and mobile-friendly design.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.
Typical timeline: 1 to 2 weeks

## Web applications
Custom web apps with user accounts, a database and payments, built with React and Next.js or Python and Flask. Example: Artisaan's Crib, a marketplace with bookings, chat and Paystack payments.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.
Typical timeline: 4 to 8 weeks

## AI agents and chatbots
AI assistants that answer questions from a business's own documents, with citations, and refuse when the answer is not in the documents. Built with Python and large language model APIs. The client pays the AI provider's usage fees directly.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.
Typical timeline: 3 to 6 weeks

## Database design and engineering
Designing, building or cleaning up databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server), including structure, security and backups.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.
Typical timeline: 1 to 3 weeks

## Data analysis and dashboards
Turning raw data into reports and visual dashboards with R, so clients can see trends and make decisions.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.
Typical timeline: 1 to 2 weeks

## Maintenance
Monthly updates, fixes, backups and monitoring after the support period ends.
Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.

## Payment terms
50% deposit before work starts, 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be paid in milestones: 50% to start, 25% at a midpoint review and 25% on delivery. Payment methods: bank transfer, Paystack, and PayPal for international clients.

## What is included
Two rounds of revisions. Setup of hosting and the domain (the client pays the hosting and domain fees directly, so they own them). Free support after delivery for 1 to 2 months, depending on the size of the project.

## What is not included
Hosting and domain fees, paid AI or third-party service fees, content writing, and new features requested after the project scope is agreed. These are quoted separately.
