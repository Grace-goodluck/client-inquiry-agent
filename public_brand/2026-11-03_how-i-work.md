# How I work with clients

## 1. Free scoping call
We start with a short call (about 30 minutes) to understand the business, the problem and what success looks like. There is no charge and no commitment.

## 2. Written proposal and quote
Within 3 working days I send a written proposal: what will be built, what is not included, the timeline, the fixed price and the payment schedule. Work starts only when both sides agree on it in writing.

## 3. Deposit and kickoff
Work begins after the 50% deposit is paid. I set up the project and share a timeline with milestones.

## 4. Building, with regular updates
I send a progress update every week, with screenshots or a preview link so the client can see real work, not just a status report. Important decisions are written down and agreed before I build them.

## 5. Review and revisions
The client reviews the work at each milestone. Two rounds of revisions are included. Changes that go beyond the agreed scope are quoted separately before any work on them begins.

## 6. Testing and launch
I test the project before launch, including on mobile, and fix any problems found. I set up hosting and the domain in the client's own accounts, so the client owns everything.

## 7. Handover and support
At launch the client receives the final payment invoice, access to all accounts and code, and simple instructions for using the product. Free support for bugs continues for 1 to 2 months after delivery, depending on the size of the project. Ongoing maintenance is available as a monthly plan.

## Communication
I work Monday to Friday and do not work on weekends. I reply to messages within 24 hours on working days. Clients can reach me by email or WhatsApp. I work from Lagos, Nigeria (West Africa Time, UTC+1), and can arrange calls across time zones for international clients.

## Who owns the work
After final payment, the client owns the website or application, its code and its accounts. I may show the project in my portfolio unless the client asks me not to.

## Refunds
Payments are non-refundable. The 50% deposit secures my time and starts the work, and later payments are made for completed milestones that the client has reviewed. If a client wants to stop a project early, they keep everything completed up to that point.
