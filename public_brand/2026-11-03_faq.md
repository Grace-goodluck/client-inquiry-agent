# TechByGrace frequently asked questions

## Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?
Yes. I work with clients in Nigeria and internationally. International clients are quoted in US dollars and can pay by PayPal. I work from Lagos (West Africa Time, UTC+1) and can arrange calls across time zones.

## Are you available for full-time roles as well as freelance projects?
Yes. I am open to full-time roles and contracts in web app development, AI engineering and agentic AI development, remote, hybrid or on-site, alongside freelance projects.

## How do I start a project with you?
Book a free scoping call. Within 3 working days I send a written proposal with the scope, timeline and fixed price. Work starts after the 50% deposit.

## Can you build an AI assistant for my business?
Yes. I build AI assistants that answer questions from a business's own documents, cite their sources, and say when they do not know rather than guessing. My Second Brain project is an example.

## Do you offer maintenance after the project?
Yes. Free support for bugs lasts 1 to 2 months after delivery, depending on project size. After that, monthly maintenance is available. Pricing depends on scope. I'll send you a quote after a short call.

## Will I own my website and its code?
Yes. After final payment, the client owns the website or app, its code and its accounts. Hosting and domains are set up in the client's own accounts.

## Do you work weekends?
No. I work Monday to Friday and reply to messages within 24 hours on working days.

## Do you offer refunds?
No. Payments are non-refundable, because the deposit reserves my time and later payments follow milestones the client has reviewed. A client who stops early keeps everything completed up to that point.
